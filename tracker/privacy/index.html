One Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

In short: One Tracker works entirely on your phone. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any of your data, with us or anyone else.

What the app reads, and why

Everything below is read and processed only on your phone, and only to show your spending to you.

  • SMS (only if you allow it) — bank, card and UPI transaction alerts, to record your spending: the last 90 days when you allow access, then new alerts as they arrive. Messages that are not payment alerts are skipped and not stored.
  • Notifications (only if you allow Notification access) — payment notifications from UPI and bank apps, to catch payments that send no SMS. Android calls this access to all notifications; every other notification, chats included, is skipped and not stored.
  • Contacts (optional, only if you allow it) — names, numbers and photos, to show a photo next to payments to people you know. Your contacts are never changed.
  • Installed payment and shopping apps — the app checks on the phone whether a short list of known apps is installed, to show their logo on payments. The list is never stored or sent.

The app can also show its own notifications (for example, a new payment), and can use your fingerprint or face to unlock through Android. It never sees your biometric data.

Where it goes

Nowhere. The app cannot connect to the internet. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.

If you use Backup, the app saves a file to a place you choose; that file is yours and the app does not send it anywhere. If you use Split, the app opens the Splitwise app with the amount and description you confirm; Splitwise's own privacy policy applies to what you save there. One Tracker is not affiliated with Splitwise or any bank.

Your control

You can turn off any access in Android settings at any time and still add expenses yourself. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it saved.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
